# data/triggers/

When a letter fires, and with what values. Owned by the director (#14).

**No prose lives here.** Two engines are kept apart: one decides *whether* a
letter is sent and with what values, the other decides *how it reads*
(`data/letters_en/`). `params` is the typed contract between them — the letter
file declares, the director supplies.

Keeping these files free of prose also keeps them free of localisation.
